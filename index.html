What it does.

Big, clear cards

High-contrast cards in a typeface made for easy reading, sized to fill your phone or tablet.

✓

Tap to move

Tap a card and it goes to the best spot. Press and hold to put it in a free cell. No dragging.

Hints that lead to a win

Tap Hint and the game works out a move that still wins. If you can no longer win from where you are, it tells you, so you can Undo.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can every game be won?

All but one of the 32,000 classic games can be won, and that one (#11982) is never dealt. You can still get stuck by your own moves. Hint tells you when that has happened, and Undo lets you back up and try another way.

Are these the same game numbers I remember?

Yes. Gide FreeCell uses the same numbered deals as the classic FreeCell, so Game #617 is the same layout it has always been. Choose New game, then Play a game by number.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in, and nothing leaves your device.

Can I make the suits easier to tell apart?

Yes. Turn on Four-color suits in Settings for blue diamonds and green clubs.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. The cards grow to fill the screen, in portrait or landscape.

How do I get support?
